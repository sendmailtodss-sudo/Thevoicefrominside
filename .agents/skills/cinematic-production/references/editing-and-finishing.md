# Editing and finishing

Use this as an editing checklist once planning or assembly is actually requested.
It is not an edit decision list for the present production.

## Editorial construction

Give each shot one narrative purpose and a concrete action. Record the actual
in/out frames, source frame rate, frame count, time base and duration. Preserve
raw masters; make derivatives with explicit crop, speed, color and codec notes.

Review the sequence at three scales: the complete arc, the action within each
shot, and the frames immediately across every seam. Rhythm should alternate
purposeful travel, discovery and readable emphasis. Do not repair a missing
story beat with an unrelated effect.

A connected camera journey needs matching perspective, position, subject scale,
direction and velocity at its join. Match-on-action, an authored threshold,
foreground occlusion, darkness, atmosphere or a motivated portal may carry the
join. A dissolve is a choice, not a universal continuity repair. Distinguish a
deliberate editorial cut from a supposedly continuous camera move.

When sound is requested, define dialogue, ambience, music and effects separately.
Use only authorized sources and keep stems. J/L cuts may bridge sound across
picture edits, but sound must not become a time source for a scroll-only visual
journey. Do not add autonomous soundtrack playback to a silent brief.

## Compositing and finishing

Define the image stack before compositing: clean plate, masks/depth, inserted
geometry/artwork, foreground occluders, atmosphere, emission, bloom, grade and
output conversion. Determine which component owns projection and color space.
Inspect contact points, silhouettes, edge softness, reflections, grain, focus
and motion blur. Check the image without bloom and without the grade.

Exact art must follow its surface: perspective and UV deformation, folds,
occlusion and material response. A knotted garment can hide and compress parts
of a print; preserving the entire flat rectangle above the knot is not a
faithful product composite. A physical stencil requires its stated preparation,
painting and removal before the revealed design becomes visible.

Keep generation, pixel corrections, effects and editorial assembly separate.
Re-render only the component whose failure has been identified. Verify that
fixing one component preserves the already accepted ones.

## Delivery

Check full playback and actual decoded frames, dimensions, color, duration and
audio streams. Derive seekable browser files with measured frequent keyframes,
fast-start metadata and tested byte-range serving. Do not set an arbitrary GOP
or resolution solely because a sample command used it.

Choose responsive composition before encoding. A mobile reframe may require a
different camera/composite rather than a crop that cuts off the action or art.
Keep complete readable artwork available in its inspection view.

Remotion's official skills and docs are useful references for frame-based
sequencing, transition overlap, matting and 3D composition. Their runtime,
license, render environment and billing are separate questions. This package
does not install or run Remotion. See the verified links in `../SOURCES.md`.
