# Award-calibrated art direction

This is an original instruction package researched and reviewed on 2026-11-12. It is informed by the companion source register, not copied from an award site or a community skill. Its purpose is to help a project earn recognition through a distinctive, meaningful, usable experience. It cannot predict a jury or guarantee an award.

## Authority and scope

Read the current user mandate, repository `AGENTS.md`, and the existing cinematic-production orchestration and relevant adoption rules. Preserve the approved writing, art, identity, languages and publication boundaries. This skill never authorizes credit use, purchases, uploads, submissions or publication. During a research or production hold, deliver only research, critique, instruction files and the handoff explicitly requested. Do not turn the research into a new storyboard, prototype, renderer or generated media.

If authorized work already covers an action, do not invent another approval loop. If a genuine permission boundary remains, explain its exact source and finish all independently authorized preparation first.

## Research standard

1. Establish today's date and the requested award cycle. Freshly open the actual official domain and the current rules. Search snippets and look-alike domains are leads, not authority.
2. Keep four evidence classes separate: **official award record**, **creator production account**, **live interaction inspected**, **proposed interpretation**. An award page or video is not a successful test of the live website. A studio's performance claim is not an independent measurement.
3. For every record save title, exact URL, publisher, inspected date, award name, award-cycle year, award date, category, score if relevant, and limitations. Separate daily, monthly, annual, public-vote and jury awards.
4. Record changed rules and archive boundaries. As freshly checked on 2026-11-12, Awwwards uses 40/30/20/10 for Design/Usability/Creativity/Content; CSSDA separates jury WOTD from public UI/UX/Innovation; FWA's current human award process differs from its legacy FOTD system frozen on 3 November 2026. Recheck these before future use.
5. Keep the corpus finite: four to six references selected for relevant problems, including at least one mission/content reference and one practical access/performance reference. Use first-party case studies when available. Do not pad the list with uninspected sites.
6. For an actual browser pass record environment, viewport, entry action, forward/back action, visited states, capture paths and failures. Inspect the captures. If only the loader appears, label it “loader inspected; full interaction unverified.” Do not infer universal failure or slowness from that observation.

## Start from meaning

State the project's actual mission and visitor tasks in plain language. Establish what the visitor should understand, feel and be able to do. List immutable source facts and creative material separately from metaphors. Do not let an award reference replace the project's own reason to exist.

For each mechanism under review, complete this sentence: “When the visitor does ___, ___ changes, which helps them understand or do ___.” If the last clause is only “see something impressive,” the mechanism needs a stronger purpose. Technical novelty receives no separate automatic credit.

Build a small comparison ledger:

| Reference | Evidence level | Useful principle | Distinctive signature we must not copy | Project-specific hypothesis | How to disprove it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Originality means a particular relation between content and action, supported by a coherent visual language. A different accent color, new shader, copied transition or longer camera flight is insufficient. Use the removal test: if bloom, photoreal textures and expensive assets disappear, does the interaction still express the idea?

## Judge the complete experience

Use the weighted rubric in [the review rubric](references/RESEARCH.md): mission 20, original interaction 20, art direction/continuity 20, spatial readability 15, agency/orientation 10, access/mobile/language 10, resilience/performance 5. This is an internal decision aid, not an official award score.

Rate each 0–4 with named evidence; leave unknowns unscored. Keep a separate artistic judgment and technical evidence column. A build passing is not artistic acceptance; a beautiful still is not continuity or usability evidence.

Apply these noncompensable gates before interpreting the total:

- **Truth:** sources, approved writing, exact art, redactions, identity and documentary context remain accurate. Synthetic reconstructions and metaphors are not represented as documentary proof.
- **Control:** the project's declared interaction contract survives stop, reverse, jump, reload at depth, resize and hidden/resume. Delayed motion cannot be excused as cinematic polish when absolute scroll is required.
- **Spatial integration:** the claimed depth is actually evidenced. Shared camera/geometry/depth or a documented camera-match and mask workflow owns perspective and occlusion. A flat plate does not supply a 3D camera or depth buffer.
- **Readability:** words have an intentional readable interval, exact glyphs and language-specific composition. Check without glow; test formation and dissolution in both directions. The visual and accessible text correspond.
- **Access:** navigation, essential content and the end task work with keyboard, touch, zoom, reduced motion, screen reader and renderer/media failure. A compelling alternative is part of the authored experience.
- **Language:** all required languages receive complete writing, controls, loading/error states, fonts and composition checks. Do not use language-independent pixels as a substitute for language support.

If any gate fails, the result is not ready regardless of spectacle or weighted total.

## Project adoption: The Voice from Inside

The current required progression is point → cosmos → brain network with luminous particle words spatially between brains → warp/Earth → real Bergen prison → creative work → one gallery. Preserve it. This skill supplies review criteria and does not create a replacement film plan.

The journey's visible state is a deterministic function of absolute native scroll, selected language, viewport composition and fixed seeds. Stop freezes; reverse reconstructs the same state; rapid jumps remain possible. No elapsed-time particle simulation, autoplay, random resampling, pointer camera, post-stop inertia or delayed scrub. Gallery/VR or a separate user-operated player may have its own explicitly bounded contract.

Norwegian, English and simplified Chinese (`zh-Hans`) require visual and semantic parity. Essential words must not exist only in particle pixels. A readable accessible alternative does not justify a poor visual experience, and a compelling visual experience does not justify excluding readers.

The strongest proposed distinction is meaningful testimony carried by spatial language while retaining precise visitor control. Treat that as a hypothesis. Do not declare global originality or superiority without comparative evidence.

## Evidence when production is later authorized

Before committing more assets, prove the riskiest requirement with the smallest authorized mechanism study. For this project, that is actual readable particle words at different depths between brains with correct occlusion and strict reversal. Do not use a particle overlay as a shortcut and call the requirement solved.

Review fixed scroll positions forward/reverse, stopping, fast jumps, loading at depth and failures. Inspect actual desktop and portrait motion, seams, readable intervals, ending and gallery handoff. Use physical mobile hardware for performance and touch claims; desktop emulation is partial evidence.

Use current [Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals) baseline thresholds with field/lab distinctions, and separately inspect frame pacing, seek latency, memory, assets and device heat. Check the full relevant accessibility standard; two automated checks do not establish conformance. The project's reduced-motion requirement is explicit; [WCAG SC 2.3.3](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html) is AAA, not AA.

Give independent reviewers concrete tasks: explain the mission after one pass, find one work, return to a known point, switch language without losing meaning, and use the low-motion route. Record misunderstandings and blockers. Compare task outcomes and remembered meaning against named references; label small qualitative studies honestly.

Close the largest gap in meaning, control or access before adding effects. Any claim of improvement must name its dimension, comparator, conditions and evidence. Do not turn a small comparison into “better UX than all this year's winners.”
