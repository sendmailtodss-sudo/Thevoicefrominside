# Award-calibrated art direction: fresh evidence, 12 November 2026

This is an independent research restart, not a confirmation of the previous research. Sources below were newly opened using the native web tool or local Chromium. The production repository was read only. No media, prototype, submission, purchase, publication or deployment was made. This report proposes a decision method; it does not claim that this project already surpasses any winner or will win an award.

## What the current evidence changes

The ambition should be to make the testimony, authorship and creative work inseparable from the experience. Awards are a calibration set, not the subject of the site. A more elaborate renderer is not evidence of a stronger idea. A beautiful cosmic passage becomes worthwhile when the audience understands more about the voice and the real work because of it.

The user's requested sequence remains the brief: point → cosmos → brain network, with luminous particle words spatially between brains → warp/Earth → real Bergen prison → creative work → a single gallery. This research does not redesign that sequence or create a new storyboard. It establishes what must later be demonstrated for the sequence to work as one authored journey.

The strongest differentiating opportunity is the conjunction of **specific human meaning, real spatial typography, complete visitor control, and equally considered access across languages and devices**. This is a design hypothesis, not a claim of global novelty. The benchmark corpus is small and cannot prove that nobody has done something similar.

## Award facts and important boundaries

- **Awwwards:** its current [evaluation page](https://www.awwwards.com/about-evaluation/) assigns 40% to Design, 30% to Usability, 20% to Creativity and 10% to Content. Its annual Site of the Year is announced in February of the following year. Thus a 2026 SOTD is a 2026 daily winner, not the 2026 annual champion. The [Developer Award](https://www.awwwards.com/developer-award/) explicitly values inclusion across devices and browsers, accessibility and mobile optimization.
- **CSSDA:** the [official explanation](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/about/) separates jury WOTD from public UI, UX and Innovation awards. WOTM and selected WOTD entries feed into WOTY. DOTY is a separate designer/studio/agency distinction. A public UX badge is not proof of being the year's best user experience. Its About page and entry FAQ differ slightly in phrasing the public-vote threshold; that operational detail should be checked again before any future submission.
- **FWA changed recently:** the fully rendered [current About page](https://thefwa.com/about) says awards require 80% yes after at least ten judgments within 21 days, with no daily quota; a founder's Golden Ticket is a separate exception. FWAi provides first-pass feedback and has no jury vote. The legacy site says it was frozen on **3 November 2026**. Do not apply its FOTD system to current awards. Fresh evidence is in `Private research evidence: fwa-about.json` and the screenshot.
- **Date labels must travel with their award:** Bruno's new portfolio has a 2026 Awwwards SOTD but a **2025** FWA of the Year in the [official legacy record](https://legacy.thefwa.com/cases/brunos-portfolio). An award announced or encountered this year is not automatically an award for this year's cycle.

## Proposed original review rubric

This is an internal 100-point rubric, **not an official award formula or prediction**. Score each dimension 0–4 and multiply by weight/4. Unknown evidence remains “unverified”; never turn it into a generous score. A scorecard only becomes meaningful when an independent reviewer can follow its evidence.

| Dimension | Weight | What strong evidence would show |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Mission and content truth | 20 | Visitors can identify the voice, the real place, the role of the creative work and the next action. Source writing, art, redactions and documentary facts remain accurate. Cosmic imagery never masquerades as factual neuroscience or a documentary image of the prison. |
| Original interaction idea | 20 | A coherent relationship between user action, changing image and changing understanding; a specific improvement over named references on the same task. The idea survives removal of bloom, expensive textures and borrowed visual tropes. |
| Art direction and cinematic continuity | 20 | A recognizable composition, material and lighting language across the entire journey; purposeful scale, framing, rhythm and handoffs; desktop and portrait compositions both intentional. The work remains strong as still images and as motion. |
| Spatial credibility and readable typography | 15 | Word particles occupy authored depths, form readable exact-language glyphs, and share appropriate camera, occlusion, perspective and atmosphere. “Between brains” is visible under inspection. Depth/debug views and effect-disabled passes support the final composite. |
| Visitor agency and orientation | 10 | Native scroll governs absolute state. Stop freezes, reverse restores, jumps work; entry, progress, language controls, navigation and the final gallery remain understandable and reachable. |
| Mobile, accessibility and language parity | 10 | Meaning and essential tasks survive touch, keyboard, reduced motion, screen reader, zoom, failed WebGL and Norwegian/English/zh-Hans. The lower-motion experience preserves authorship and emotional intent. |
| Performance and delivery resilience | 5 | Measured start, input and frame behavior on stated devices; semantic content is usable before heavy assets. Failures yield a usable alternative. No claims based solely on a desktop lab score. |

Scale: 0 absent or harmful; 1 generic or materially broken; 2 coherent but ordinary/incomplete; 3 distinctive and convincingly evidenced; 4 unusually resolved, with direct comparative evidence. Do not flatten a critical failure into an average. Content integrity, meaningful access, essential controls, language completeness, truthful spatial integration and the strict motion contract are **gates**. Any failed gate means “not ready,” regardless of total.

The spatial typography gate is especially concrete: a glowing flat label cannot satisfy the requirement merely because it travels across a film. A filmed plate needs a verified camera/depth/mask workflow; a true 3D world needs coherent camera and geometry ownership. At selected absolute positions, test front/behind relationships with effects disabled. Test word formation, full readability and dissolution in both directions. Essential words must also exist as accessible text.

## A finite decision process

1. **Confirm scope and truth:** preserve the existing brief and production hold. Audit what is approved writing, actual artwork, documentary reference and synthetic imagery. Make no production assets during research.
2. **Choose the comparison question:** for each proposed signature mechanism, name a visitor need and one or two relevant references. Ask what should become clearer, more memorable or easier, and what evidence could disprove the claim.
3. **Extract a principle, discard the signature:** record each reference's useful principle, its nontransferable visual identity and its verification limits. A matrix of shaders or libraries is insufficient.
4. **Review the whole intended experience:** judge meaningful connections across the user-specified sequence, accessibility and the single gallery, not only the strongest opening frame. This is a future review gate, not a new storyboard now.
5. **When production is later authorized, prove the hardest claim first:** the particle words' spatial readability and deterministic reversal must be demonstrated before further asset spending. This reduces the chance of buying beautiful material that cannot support the required interaction.
6. **Run comparative critique:** have independent reviewers describe the mission, find a work, return to a known point and explain the signature interaction. Compare recorded behavior and recall, not adjectives. Label small studies qualitative; do not invent statistical certainty.
7. **Close the largest meaningful gap:** prioritize broken meaning, access or spatial truth before adding effects. Report “stronger than reference X on task Y under conditions Z” only when evidence supports it. Never claim superior UX to all 2026 winners from a short research sample.

Performance reference: [Google's current Web Vitals guidance](https://web.dev/articles/vitals) gives LCP ≤2.5 s, INP ≤200 ms and CLS ≤0.1 at the 75th percentile, segmented by mobile/desktop. These are useful baseline outcomes, not complete measures of a WebGL journey; also measure frame pacing, seek delay, memory and thermal behavior on real devices. [W3C's motion guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html) treats disabling nonessential interaction animation as SC 2.3.3 **AAA**; honoring it here is an explicit project requirement. [Reflow](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html) is **AA**, with a 320-CSS-pixel vertical-content condition and defined exceptions. Do not mislabel AAA as AA or claim conformance from these two checks alone.

## Evidence and limits

Local Chromium was used because this executor offered no shared-desktop control tool. This is headless desktop observation, not a physical-device test. The browser evidence folder contains rendered award text, direct source URLs, timestamps and screenshots. Screenshots were actually inspected; three live reference sites initially remained at loaders/entry UI. A subsequent ORVAE pass reached its architectural entry reveal. That limited observation does not establish full interactive behavior, nor does it prove the other sites generally fail or are slow for users. No comparative performance measurements were made.

The native search tool returned misleading look-alike award domains and unrelated third-party research. Those were not adopted as authority. Old internal research was not read as factual evidence. The annual Awwwards listing and Igloo case-study pages failed in the native fetch; neither is used to assert an annual 2026 winner or a verified interaction.
