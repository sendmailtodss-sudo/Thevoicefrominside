# Site direction (owner decisions, 13 November 2026)

These are decisions the owner has already made. Apply them without asking again. Ask only when a
choice below is genuinely open. Never remove or disable existing content as a side effect; see
"Additive changes must not alter existing parts" in `AGENTS.md`.

## Two directions, kept apart
- **Stemmen / The Voice**: activism, social criticism, system reform, accountability. Holds
  «Dagbok fra innsiden», the book, press and the legal tools.
- **Fra innsiden / From Inside**: prevention, aftercare, the two forums (inmates with aftercare
  focus; relatives), and the art community with gallery, music and shop.
- The two never share a menu. Dagbok and the art community belong to different sides.

## Navigation
- One dropdown menu per direction, defined on that direction's main page and repeated unchanged on
  every page of that direction.
- A menu choice goes straight to the text or the tool. No extra collection page with cards or text
  boxes in between. Keep the number of pages to the minimum the menu needs.
- Dagbok entries appear only in the dropdown, titled by inmate number then title.
- Legal tools, art, music: same principle, straight to the tool.
- Remove links and text that do not serve the chosen page.
- No separate "Menu" or language buttons in page headers. Language is chosen with the three flags
  on the landing page (Norwegian left, Chinese centre, English right) and applies to all text.
- Top of every page: only the living symbol, looking and moving as on the landing page. It leads
  back to the very beginning. The words «Stemmen fra innsiden» sit in the top left corner.

## Landing page
- Background: a real-looking universe in the style of NASA imagery (stars, nebulae, planets), no
  Earth in view, very high resolution, not cartoon-like. Show the owner real reference images
  before recreating it.
- Brains: small, many, moving continuously into the depth of the universe, with visible synapse
  activity between them, like the former sequence between the Milky Way and the warp tunnel but
  much smaller. Front brains must not be large.
- Symbol: centred above the title; must look like real diamond material with the gold star clearly
  inside while it turns. Title: pure gold with crisp contours and real glints. It must look real.
- Stone tablet: also presents the book, press and the legal tools.
- The scroll journey after the hero stays exactly as the latest approved version.

## Art side
- Remove the old 3D gallery.
- «Billedkunst» in the menu opens a gallery wall like the one in the new film: frames with the works.
- Choices on one side, as clear gold-edged buttons: all works, choose by inmate number, «Vis i VR»,
  and «Om kunstneren» (opens the text the artist chose).
- VR gallery must look like the gallery in the new film. Recessed speakers stand between the
  frames and are almost frame-sized, slightly smaller.
- Menu also holds contact/collaboration and «Om galleriet».
- Shop: models must match the new films (Paris man, Bergen woman) and the print must sit in the
  fabric exactly as in the films.

## Working method
- Go through every page proactively against this direction; fix what plainly follows from it.
- Keep Norwegian, English and Simplified Chinese complete on everything that is built.
- Publish in small steps and verify the live result each time.
