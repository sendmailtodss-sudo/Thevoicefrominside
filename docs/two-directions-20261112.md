# Two directions and cinematic finishing

The shared home offers two distinct directions: editorial writing and legal tools, and art, creative collaboration and planned forums. Each direction has its own navigation and a return to the home menu. Diary dropdowns use the verified dates and contributor aliases available in this developer distribution; full production diary content is managed separately.

The final visual journey removes the DNA and CCTV chapters, places music before the courtyard artwork, and ends by holding the LA interior frame before closing to the original point. New finished media retain their source-specific draft descriptions. The gallery uses six approved original images inside a subset of its frames; most spaces remain empty. The LA wall uses the original LAR image through a planar composite. These are illustrative scenes and are not architectural surveys, production garment designs, or evidence of operational services.

Entry pages have user-operated silent short films and reduced-motion alternatives. Background light on reading pages follows scrolling rather than an autonomous animation clock. The Chinese interface labels are included in this contributor checkout; the separately maintained full production editorial translation supplement is intentionally excluded along with production case documents and manuscripts.

Changing these browser interfaces does not enable real accounts, identity verification, payments, public submissions or forum membership. Existing code, writing and artwork rights remain separate; no new licence is granted by this change.
