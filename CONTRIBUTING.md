# Contributing

Developers, designers, artists, translators, accessibility testers and careful reviewers are welcome. No connection to prison is required.

Start with [the development guide](docs/development.md). Choose a focused issue or describe the problem your change solves. Use synthetic examples rather than real case files or private personal details.

Before opening a pull request:

1. Run `npm test` and `npm run build`.
2. Check the affected pages locally, including mobile width and keyboard access.
3. Describe the behavior before and after, what you tested and any remaining limits.
4. Keep private documents, credentials, production bindings, contact logs and personal identities out of the change and its history.

A working prototype does not authorize enabling payments, submissions, messaging or publication. Preserve disabled states until the maintainer explicitly approves and verifies the supporting service.

Editorial writing, translations, credited artwork and approved redactions need their own review. Do not replace a person's account with invented facts or remove redactions. Artwork ownership, permission to display an image and permission to sell reproductions are separate permissions.

This repository does not grant a new blanket license to the book, artwork or other project content. Ask the maintainer about reuse terms for the particular material. Contributor access or a pull request does not confer production administration or ownership of third-party work.
