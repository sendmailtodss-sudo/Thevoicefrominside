Who should be invited as counsel?

Indicate whom the project should consider inviting when the analyzed material and confirmed available funds justify approaching counsel. This is an advisory preference.

Preliminary analysis and fundraising can develop in parallel before a lawyer assesses an engagement. This preference process does not block that work.

The result advises whom to invite. It creates no retainer, releases no money and establishes no legal finding. Counsel must assess conflicts, capacity, scope and terms independently.

How it works

  1. Read each official professional profile and whether the candidate is merely proposed, has been invited or has expressed interest.
  2. Use the website’s existing sign-in. Each account may have one current preference in this list version.
  3. You can change or withdraw your choice while the list is open. A new list version starts a new ballot without carrying old votes forward.
  4. When the material and funds are sufficient, the project can use these preferences to invite prospective counsel. Any engagement requires a separate agreement.

What is visible?

Only account counts per candidate and in total are public. These are not counts of independently verified, distinct people. The website stores the account ID, candidate, list version and change time to enforce one current choice. This feature does not display or store your email address.

The lawyer controlling the fundraising account has a separate role. A choice here changes neither account authority nor the website’s access to money.
