Art as peaceful protest

One voice.
Many expressions.

Talent from inside

Art can express what is difficult to say. Here, creative work gets space — and people get another chance to be seen.

Look closer. Find a work that speaks to you. Let art start conversations and bring people together.

Original artwork

One work. One physical original.

An original is listed only with the artist’s declaration of the single physical work. The artist sets a fixed price, and the work is reserved when the purchase is processed. An artist’s declaration and an independent review are different and must be labelled clearly.

  • Available Listed by the artist.
  • Reserved Held while the purchase is completed.
  • Sold The original has an owner.
  • Not listed No physical original is offered.

The book images in this exhibition are AI-assisted illustrations. They are not listed as unique physical originals.

Prints and wearable art

Take the art into everyday life.

Everyone is welcome to browse and buy when sales open. Choose an artwork and preview it on a T-shirt. The artist separately authorises reproductions; submitting to the gallery does not automatically grant a sales licence.

The proposed T-shirt has one large artwork on the back. The final price and delivery terms must be shown before purchase.

Reproductions may bring an artist attention and income. They are separate from the original work and do not guarantee that its value will increase.

Create. Stand together. Begin again.

A community for people with something to express.

Everyone is welcome to contribute art and music when the programme opens. You can choose a name or artist name. Contributions must be your own, have the necessary rights and be approved through moderation.
