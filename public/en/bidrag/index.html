What is stored and what is public?

Your profile and contributions are linked to your sign-in. Your display name, social profile link and approved contributions are public. You choose whether to use your own name or an artist name.

Pending contributions are visible only to you and the editorial team. Reports are private. Likes are stored per signed-in account; share counts measure sharing actions. You can delete your own work from its contribution page.
