What these numbers show

Page views count registered page openings. Visits count browser tab sessions, not verified individual people. Refreshing a page can increase page views; opening a new tab can count as a new visit.

Share actions count clicks on channel buttons, successful link copies and completed hand-offs to the device’s share menu. They do not confirm that a post was published. Shares made outside this website and private messages cannot all be detected.

Referred visits use the channel tag in shared links or the referring website when available. Missing referrals appear as direct / unknown. Bots, blocked scripts and manipulated requests can affect the numbers.

Measurement begins with this feature. No historical visits or shares have been reconstructed. Opening this statistics page does not count as a visit or page view. Browser-only measurement storage lasts for the tab session; no names, email addresses, IP addresses or user-agent strings are saved in the analytics database.
