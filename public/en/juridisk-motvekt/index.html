Legal counterweight

Three tools to examine, document and formulate questions about a case.

  • Rettspeil — examine a decision and the review method.
  • inmAIte — work with documents and prepare a complaint draft in one workspace.
  • Access to records — find templates for requesting records.

These tools do not submit complaints or access requests. External case intake and payments are closed; deadlines and legal questions require human review.
