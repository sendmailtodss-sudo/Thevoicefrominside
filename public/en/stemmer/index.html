How a future contribution should be handled

  1. The author decides what to share and for which purpose.
  2. A moderator checks identifying details, source status and allegations.
  3. Those facing serious, specific allegations have an opportunity to respond before publication.
  4. Approved text has a date, source description and a visible correction route.

We want transparency and accountability. Threats, public targeting of private people and information about children must not be published. A story must not be used in litigation without separate agreement with the person concerned and the responsible lawyer.
