For individuals

Start with one thing.

Test a page on your phone. Describe an accessibility issue. Review a translation or propose an improvement with a clear reason. Small, specific contributions are valuable.

Share knowledge and opportunities.

Professional groups, newsrooms, cultural organisations and community organisations can help with source review, accessibility, guidance or creative collaboration. Describe what you can offer and which conditions need agreement.

Sources and expert review

Strengthen the evidence.

Help with documentation, methods or legal review. Distinguish personal claims, what a source supports and what remains unresolved. Do not put private case documents in public GitHub issues.

Explore the tools and method →

Collaboration starts with agreement.

  1. Describe the contributionWhat would you like to improve, create or investigate? Include enough public information for others to understand the proposal.
  2. Agree the scopeAgree tasks, credits, rights and contact arrangements before work is shared or used.
  3. Try and reviewTest a focused result and make it understandable to the next contributor.

The public GitHub project is an entry point for collaboration. Hosting, private sources and operational information are handled separately. Proposing a contribution creates no automatic agreement, fee or sponsor status.
