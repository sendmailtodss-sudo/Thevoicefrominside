Creative community · collaboration and support

Give creativity
a way forward.

A work of art, a song or a clothing collection can begin with restless energy and become something that matters to others. We invite people, organisations and foundations to build opportunities together.

Everyone is welcome. You do not need a connection to prison, a finished project or an established artist name.

Organisations · foundations · professional communities

Open a door.

A studio, a mentor, recording time, materials, an exhibition or help with production and distribution can give an idea direction. We welcome cultural organisations, youth and reintegration services, businesses and foundations offering concrete opportunities.

Propose a collaboration or support arrangement. Tasks, costs, rights and any sponsor visibility are agreed with the people involved before work starts.

Artists · musicians · creators

You do not have to create alone.

Find art and music that move you. Offer mentoring, mixing, film, design or production. Explore how a work can become a print or part of a clothing line, and how sharing can help more voices be heard.
