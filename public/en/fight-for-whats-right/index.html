  1. The accountWhat does the person say they experienced? Preserve their own words and distinguish their account from other accounts.
  2. The documentsWhich source supports which statement? Identify the document, quotation and date where known. Include conflicting evidence.

These tools do not send complaints or access requests for you. External case intake, payments and automatic legal conclusions are not enabled. Have drafts reviewed by a qualified person where the case requires it.
