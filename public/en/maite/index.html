MAITE · working title

From a pile of documents
to an organised case.

Check your documents, keep track of dates and prepare a draft you can review yourself.

Private prototype for preparing complaints and tracking deadlines. The case record stays in this tab. PDF and image extraction sends the file to a local helper process on your own machine, if you have started it. No text is sent to an AI service or added to Rettsspeil’s aggregate statistics. Use synthetic documents or documents cleared for testing.

The text review uses fixed search rules. It provides questions to check and source references, not a measured probability of a legal breach. No deadline is calculated automatically. Save your work before closing or reloading the page.

Resume from a private working copy

Open a password-protected .sfi work copy or an existing unencrypted JSON work file. Decryption and validation happen locally. A forgotten password cannot be recovered. A structured case report is not a resumable work file.

Nothing is replaced until the entire file and the original files’ checksums have been checked. A valid import can still contain inaccurate claims or a self-review you have not performed. Review the contents before using them.

Switching cases must never mix documents or drafts from two cases. This is local separation, not an account service for multiple clients.
