Build the evidence base and funding in parallel

Three phases – legal assessment when the basis is ready

  1. 1. Collect and analyse

    Decisions receive preliminary analysis while contributions are collected by the lawyer responsible for the account. Each decision can count as machine-analysed without prior legal assessment. Account administration and reconciliation are a separate engagement.

  2. 2. Invite an assessment

    Once enough distinct decisions have received preliminary analysis and a current account report confirms sufficient available funds, a lawyer can be invited to assess the material. No invitation is sent automatically.

  3. 3. Independent legal assessment

    The lawyer decides whether there are grounds for an engagement and whether to accept it. Later use of funds requires a mandate, agreed terms and a budget. A full progress indicator is not a legal conclusion.

Where does the review stand?

Machine analysis, human checking and legal assessment are shown separately. A decision counts as fully machine-analysed when every planned checkpoint has machine-analysis provenance. Prior legal assessment is not required. Missing documents may remain unresolved, and a flag only means that something should be checked. Retained machine flags may later concern the same points that receive human or legal review; these counts must not be added together.

Step 1 · in this browser tab

Understand a decision

Fixed search rules look for Norwegian wording and find potentially relevant excerpts and questions. The interface is translated; your source text stays unchanged. This is not an AI analysis or legal assessment, and a missing match does not establish non-compliance. The tool does not determine guilt, motive, appeal deadlines or compensation claims.

Have several documents, a PDF or a scanned letter? Open the new MAITE case workspace for a document register, local OCR, receipt dates, chronology and drafts. This simpler text review keeps its own workspace.

The text is not sent to a server or AI provider. This page uses no analytics service or browser storage for case content. “Clear” removes content from the interface and program references. Secure erasure from the device, browser memory, extensions or your original files cannot be guaranteed.

The note may contain your excerpts and comments. It is not anonymised and must not be published. Downloading sends nothing.

What about automatic AI analysis?

Open the analysis workshop for multiple documents, dated legal texts and checks of structured model results against exact quotations. A separate local runner has been built for a selected AI provider, but requires an API key and explicit approval of the content before transmission. No real cases have been sent and no model account is connected. Preliminary machine analysis does not require prior legal assessment. Source checking does not establish legal correctness, and private results do not automatically enter public statistics. “Maximum effort” or a long processing time does not guarantee a correct answer.

Step 2 · synthetic test data only

Example institution breakdown

This is a separate method exercise with its own synthetic cases, not a breakdown of the progress figures above. Statistics can show which checkpoints need further work. They cannot alone establish that anyone deliberately broke the law. Test material is grouped by institution, broad function and year, without names or prisoner numbers.

Each case counts once

Several decisions or uploads from one case must be reconciled before counting. Duplicate checking is demonstrated with fixed test IDs. Linking real cases requires a separate, controlled process.

Small counts are withheld

At least 10 cases per visible cell. The entire category breakdown is withheld where a small cell could otherwise be calculated. Fixed releases and restrictions on small changes limit inference between reports. This does not guarantee anonymity.

Counterevidence matters too

Completeness, document versions, appeals and information supporting the decision must accompany private review. A self-selected sample cannot show how common an error is across the correctional service.

Funding alongside analysis

Legal assistance – funds held by the lawyer

The plan is for contributions to go directly to a suitable account controlled only by the project's account lawyer, if the lawyer and bank accept the arrangement. The lawyer may take on account administration and reconciliation before a later engagement to assess litigation. The website, project owner and AI tool must not be able to withdraw or transfer money.

No fundraising is open. Account administration, authority over funds and contribution terms must be settled separately. There is no account number or payment button here. The server report and local demonstration below are clearly separated.

Funds held by the lawyer

The lawyer confirms receipts against the bank statement and keeps records under the agreed arrangement. A pledge does not count as money received.

A defined engagement

Funds may only pay for agreed legal assistance. Once sufficient analysed material and confirmed funding are available, a lawyer is invited to assess the material, a possible engagement and its budget. A case count or AI assessment cannot start proceedings or trigger payment.

Returning unused funds

Terms must specify deadlines, costs and when funds must be returned. The system can prepare a refund list when the conditions are met. The lawyer makes the transfers and confirms them against the bank.

What you should be able to consider before participating

The first real pilot must be limited to adults aged 18 or over. Participation must be voluntary and separate from decisions about imprisonment, treatment or benefits. Declining or withdrawing must have no adverse consequences. Other people's information, particularly children's, requires separate assessment even where the submitter is an adult.

  1. Understand the purpose. The study must test questions using an open method and also record findings that do not support the suspicion.
  2. Check the documents. Keep your originals. Distinguish personal experience, the document's wording and assessment. Appeal deadlines must be handled independently of the project.
  3. Understand what may be shared. Private review is separate from permission to enter a study or publish material.
  4. Choose any later contact separately. A contact link makes information pseudonymised, not anonymous. An independent custodian must control that link. Contact information must not appear in public statistics.
  5. A new request if a case becomes possible. A lawyer must check originals and seek fresh consent and an engagement before information is used in a specific case. Nobody automatically becomes a party to litigation.
  6. Understand withdrawal and retention. It must be clear what can be withdrawn, what has already been aggregated and published, and how long each type of information is retained.
Participation is not open. No checkbox on this page activates real collection. A legal basis for processing health information and criminal-offence data, among other types, must be assessed separately. Consent alone does not resolve every requirement.
Before a public launch

    The project owner must approve the working version. A verifiable method, security review and defined operational responsibility must precede real collection.
