Two distinct rooms

Space to be understood.

We propose separate rooms because people in or formerly in prison may have different questions about prison, release and life afterwards, while relatives also need space for their own burdens and support. Nobody should have to share their story in the other room. The examples and membership are fictional; you cannot sign in, read real posts or submit anything here. A future forum must be designed with privacy in mind, but this sketch offers no guarantee of confidentiality.

Moving forward together

Experience can become a helping hand.

We want to explore how people who have found a new direction can become mentors, and how organisations working in prevention and reintegration might participate. No organisations are presented as partners in this preview.

Before a real community can open, access, privacy and moderation need to be designed. Membership in this preview is illustrative only; we do not ask for identification, documentation or personal information here.
