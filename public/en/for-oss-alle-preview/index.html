Two distinct rooms

Space to be understood.

We propose separate rooms because people in or formerly in prison may have different questions about prison, release and life afterwards, while relatives also need space for their own burdens and support. Nobody should have to share their story in the other room. The examples and membership are fictional. You can try the application and moderator workflow, then add local example replies; nothing is submitted to a service. A future forum must be designed with privacy in mind, but this sketch offers no guarantee of confidentiality.

    01 / PRISON AND THE WAY FORWARD

    The way forward

    For people in or formerly in prison who want to share experience and find support before and after release. Participation during a sentence would depend on permitted access or authorised support; this does not imply internet access in prison.

    Moving forward together

    Experience can become a helping hand.

    We want to explore how people who have found a new direction can become mentors, and how organisations working in prevention and reintegration might participate. No organisations are presented as partners in this preview.
