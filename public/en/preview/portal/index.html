About us

Two directions,
a shared wish for change.

We want to make room for art and creativity, and for people who need community and support after prison. We envision separate rooms because people in or formerly in prison may want to share experiences of prison and moving forward, while relatives need space for their own questions, burdens and support. Nobody should have to share their story in the other room. The aim is to make opportunities and support easier to reach, including outside the largest cities.

At the same time, we want to tell stories about life inside prison and ask critical questions about the system. These two directions share creators but have different purposes. Some organisations depend on working with the system to help people. We therefore want to make clear that supporting or working with the art community and forum does not imply support for activism or every view in the other direction.

We welcome dialogue with organisations and people with lived experience. The community and mentorship idea are still in development. This division describes the website's content; a formal organisational structure has not been established here.
