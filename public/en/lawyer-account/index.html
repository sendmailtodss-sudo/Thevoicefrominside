Spleis / separate lawyer account report

Shared legal support. Visible figures.

Follow two separate records: the dated gross amount on Spleis and the lawyer’s confirmed account report.

Fundraising and preliminary analysis can develop before a lawyer decides whether to take a case. A funding goal is not a legal assessment or an automatic instruction to bring a lawsuit.

The lawyer controls the account

The proposed arrangement is for only the appointed lawyer to control the account. The website displays aggregate figures and cannot move money. The actual account arrangements and mandate must be confirmed separately with the lawyer and the bank.

A report, not online banking

The account reporter confirms the figures when recording them. The website does not check them directly against the bank. No donor information, account numbers or private accounting explanations are shown here.

WorkshopRettsspeil (Norwegian)For the account reporter

Contributions can open through a verified Spleis link only after the recipient and account arrangements are confirmed. This website cannot receive or transfer money.
