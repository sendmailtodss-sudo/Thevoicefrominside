ROOM FOR YOUR EXPRESSION

What is
your idea?

You do not need a studio, a record label or a particular background to begin.

Use your own name or an artist name. You choose what you want to share about yourself.

  • Poetry and writing
  • Music and recordings
  • Video and performance
  • Painting and drawing
  • Photography
  • Digital prints
  • AI art
  • Sculpture and physical works

Keep the physical original safely at home. Use your phone to take a sharp photograph in even light, or film the work from several angles. Keep a copy at its original quality.

Prepare a contribution →

New submissions are not open. You can prepare a local draft in the workshop. It shows supported file types and current availability; no draft is sent automatically.

MUSIC / VIDEO / COLLABORATION

A song does not
have to stand alone.

Looking for someone to mix, produce, record or make a video? The music room is intended for tracks and a way to describe what a project needs when the service opens.

When the service is open, one signed-in account can support several different songs, once per song. Collaboration offers must respect the artist’s contact settings and be approved before contact details are shared.

YOUR WORK / YOUR CHOICES

Three things that belong together.
Three different rights.

The physical original

The painting, drawing or sculpture remains yours. You can exhibit it digitally and keep it at home. A future original sale must have one clear state: available, reserved or sold.

The image or recording

What you upload is a display copy, with its own credit and permission to publish. A photograph of a work does not transfer ownership of the original.

Prints and reproductions

T-shirts, wallpaper, wall stencils and other prints need a separate agreement. Artists choose the uses, price and minimum compensation. Viewing or sharing a work does not automatically grant commercial rights.

Try the artwork before deciding.

Choose one back artwork with the artist’s signature. On the front: a small fist over the heart and the approved website address 5 cm above the hem. You can choose community branding on an inside label only.

The preview is a placement sketch. Physical measurements, licensing, production prices and the supplier must be confirmed before an order. No payment is made here.
